# Rhinestoone

A job board that costs a little to use — on purpose.

---

**The idea:** free job applications get spammed. Rhinestoone asks applicants to stake a small deposit, set by whoever posted the job, before their application counts. It's not a huge barrier, just enough that a real person decided this application was worth sending.

## What it does

- Anyone can **post a job** and decide how much of a stake applicants need to put up.
- Applicants **apply with a stake** — one application per job, no spam, no duplicates.
- Posters can **shortlist** candidates directly, a simple flag visible to everyone.
- What happens to the stake afterward — refunded back to the applicant, or kept as a platform fee — is a single policy the platform admin sets for everyone. It's not decided job-by-job, so applicants always know the rule going in.

There's no escrow of a salary or payment here — Rhinestoone isn't a payroll or contract tool. It's purely about making the *application* step mean something.
